Is Maldini a superforecaster?

1,500 predictions from 2022-Q4 to 2026-Q2. Final data from 11 May 2026.

@mundomaldini is a Spanish football journalist who publishes weekly match predictions. Predictions are evaluated with Brier scores (lower = more accurate). A superforecaster scores Brier < 0.20. Naive baseline: 0.2222 (33% each H/D/A).

Verdict

Lower is better. Over 1,500 scored predictions Maldini averages 0.1916: under the 0.20 superforecaster bar, and level with betting markets at about 0.19. Giving each outcome a third scores 0.2222.

Quarters

His worst quarter is 2023-Q1 and his best is the next one, 2023-Q2. From then on the bars stay near or under the 0.20 line.

Competitions

LaLiga holds 909 of his predictions, at 0.1952, and the Champions League 420, at 0.1861. Only the Eurocopa, the Mundial and the Nations League score above 0.20, on 58 predictions together: too few to call him worse at national-team football.

Recent matches

Single matches are noisy. Atlético–Celta at 60% home ended in an away win (0.4200); Betis–Oviedo at 70% home went home (0.0467). That is why the verdict uses the average over 1,500.
